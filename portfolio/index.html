About

Prasham Shah

I am a Chartered Accountant (ICAI) and MBA graduate from UCLA Anderson with over seven years of experience spanning investment banking at Bank of America Securities, private equity at Apex Capital Growth Partners, management consulting at Deloitte, and M&A advisory at KPMG.

Across my career I have worked on transactions ranging from $72 million to $5.2 billion across sectors including technology, steel, food distribution, geothermal energy, natural gas, and education. I am a CFA Level II candidate and have studied at LSE London and in the Netherlands.

Having built my career across San Francisco, Los Angeles, and Mumbai, I am now relocating to India to pursue buy-side private fund and private equity roles — bringing a global perspective to one of the world's most dynamic investment markets.
